\chapter*{Appendix E: Glossary of Terms}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Appendix E: Glossary of Terms}
\markboth{Appendix E: Glossary of Terms}{Appendix E: Glossary of Terms}

\textbf{Digital Alibi.} Evidence of a board's information position at the moment a specific AI-assisted decision was made: the four elements below, present simultaneously, captured contemporaneously, in a form that survives forensic examination. An alibi is not a defence; it is evidence of position at the moment a specific event occurred.

\textbf{Decision Identity.} The first element: which AI system, at which version, with which parameters, produced which output, in which operational context, at which moment.

\textbf{The Information Picture.} The second element: the complete set of data, context, model state, and external conditions present and active at the output moment, including what the system could not see.

\textbf{The Human Oversight Record.} The third element: who reviewed the AI output, what they were shown, what authority they held to override, and what they decided, recorded at the time.

\textbf{The Accountability Chain.} The fourth element: named individual accountability from the AI system to the board, documented at the moment of the decision, not assigned retrospectively.

\textbf{Negative Space.} The structural absences in an information environment: the data a system was not given, the context it was not designed to consider, the inputs it was blind to. Evidentially as significant as the positive record.

\textbf{Governance Address.} The organisation's position across the five facets of the Governance Classification, expressed as A{[}1-5{]}·E{[}1-5{]}·C{[}1-5{]}·R{[}1-5{]}·M{[}1-5{]}. A location, not a score.

\textbf{Material AI-Assisted Decision.} A decision produced or materially influenced by an AI system whose consequences engage the organisation's regulatory obligations, fiduciary duties, or exposure to litigation: credit, underwriting, claims, hiring, procurement, and compliance determinations among them.

\textbf{Output Moment.} The moment an AI system produces its result. The only moment at which the model version, inputs, parameters, and negative space are simultaneously fixed, and therefore the only moment at which the Digital Alibi can be captured.

\textbf{Shadow AI.} AI systems deployed within an organisation without formal governance approval, typically productivity tools adopted at team level or features embedded in already-licensed software. A board governance problem, because the approved framework does not cover systems the board does not know exist.

\textbf{Agentic AI.} AI systems that take actions rather than produce recommendations, operating autonomously across extended decision sequences within a pre-authorised permission set. Agentic deployment multiplies decision density and makes any evidentiary gap accumulate at machine speed.

\textbf{Decision Map.} The governance architecture specifying the six-field contemporaneous record to be captured for each material AI-assisted decision.

\textbf{Alibi Record.} The output of the Decision Map architecture for a specific decision: the contemporaneous, tamper-evident, retrievable record of the complete information picture at the output moment.

\textbf{Retrievability Certification.} Confirmation that a decision record is complete and producible within the timeframe a regulatory or legal examination would require.
